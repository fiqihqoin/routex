# Sandbox Testing

Before Going Live, you are required to perform thorough testing in the sandbox.

## How to Simulate Payment

Since the sandbox doesn't use real money, you can simulate payment status through the portal.

1. Generate a QRIS in the sandbox.
2. Open the [Sandbox Portal](https://app.routex.web.id/portal/sandbox).
3. Find your transaction and click the **Simulate Success** button.
4. Check if your system receives the webhook correctly.

# Retry & DLQ

If your server fails to receive a webhook (returns a status other than 200), Routex will attempt to resend it.

## Retry Policy

Routex will attempt a maximum of **10 retries** with exponential intervals:
1. 1 minute
2. 5 minutes
3. 15 minutes
4. ... up to 24 hours

## Dead Letter Queue (DLQ)

If it still fails after 10 attempts, the webhook will enter the DLQ. You can view and trigger manual resends through the Routex Dashboard.
